Good to know

Questions guests ask

What are your opening hours?

We are open every day from 6:00 AM – 11:00 PM.

Do you deliver food?

Yes. Delivery is free within Kohalpur. Choose your dishes on the menu page and send the order on WhatsApp, or call 9858085412.

How do I pay for a food order?

You pay when you receive your order, or at the counter if you eat in or take away.

How much are the rooms?

Rooms are Rs 1,500 or Rs 1,800 per night, depending on the room. Check-out is by 12:00 noon.

How do I book a room or an event?

Send a request with the booking form or call us. A room or event booking is confirmed once we agree the details and receive an advance payment.

How many guests can you host?

Our hall seats up to 25 guests and the dining area another 25. A new building with a hall for more than 100 guests is coming soon.

Where are you?

We are at Kohalpur-11, Newroad, Banke, Nepal. Open the location in Google Maps (opens in a new tab).
